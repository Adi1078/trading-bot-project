How it works

The bot automatically runs the configured Chartink screeners every 5 minutes during market hours and reads back the matching stocks. For each stock that is also in your Watchlist, it places a trade using the configuration below. Stocks not in the watchlist are silently skipped, and each stock is traded at most once per day.

No setup is needed on Chartink — the screeners are built into the bot.
